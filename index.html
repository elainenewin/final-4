Gel Electrophoresis

Load your DNA samples into the wells, set the voltage, and watch the bands separate.

DNA has a negative charge. When electricity flows through the gel, DNA moves toward the positive end. Smaller fragments travel faster — farther from the wells. This lets us identify and compare DNA sizes.

Centrifuge Simulator

Load sample tubes, set RPM, and watch the components separate by density.

Centrifuges spin samples at high speed, creating a centrifugal force that separates particles by density. Heavier particles move to the bottom. This is used to isolate DNA, cells, proteins, and more.
